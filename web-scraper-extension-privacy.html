Is your scraper extension sending your data?

A scraper extension sees every page you point it at, and often every page you visit. Extensions change hands, and a new owner can change what one collects. You don't need to trust anyone's word: here is a ten-minute check you can run on any of them, Magic Scraper included.

A ten-minute check for any extension

01

Read the permissions

In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, press Details. "Read and change all your data on all websites" means it can see every page you open, not just the one you scrape.

02

Read the privacy disclosures

On the store listing, open Privacy practices. Look for "Web history", "Personally identifiable information" or "Website content" being collected.

03

Watch its traffic

Open the page, press F12, choose Network, then scrape. Requests to a domain that isn't the site you're on are the extension talking to someone.

04

Check who owns it now

Look at the publisher on the listing and the developer's website. When a popular extension is sold, the name stays the same and the owner changes.

05

Look for consent screens

A screen asking you to "accept all" before the tool works is asking for something. Read what.

Why it matters for scraping

Scraping work is often confidential: a prospect list, a competitor's prices, research nobody has published yet. A tool that sends the pages you visit, or your results, to its own servers or an AI model is sharing that work, whatever its terms say about it.

Reasonable questions

Are web scraper extensions safe?

Some are and some collect more than you'd expect. Check the permissions, the store's privacy disclosures and the network requests while you scrape, and look at who owns the extension now.

Does Magic Scraper send my data anywhere?

No. Scraped data stays in your browser until you save a file. Detection and export make no network requests. The only requests are further pages of the site you're crawling, and a one-time licence check with Gumroad if you buy Pro.

Why does a scraper extension need access to all websites?

To work on whatever site you open, and to crawl pages in the background. In Magic Scraper that wider access is optional and requested only when a feature needs it.

How do I see what an extension sends?

Open DevTools with F12, choose the Network tab, and use the extension. Any request to a domain other than the site you're on came from somewhere else, often the extension.

Does Magic Scraper use AI or send pages to a server?

No. Detection runs in your browser with no AI service and no server of ours.

What happens when an extension is sold?

The listing usually keeps its name and users, and the new owner can change what it collects in an update. Check the publisher name and the privacy disclosures after a big update.
