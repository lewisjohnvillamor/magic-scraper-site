Thank you — now paste your key.

Your licence is already active. It just has to be told to the extension.

Your licence key is in your Gumroad receipt, emailed the moment the payment cleared. If it is not in your inbox within a minute or two, check the spam folder — that is where mail from a payment processor tends to land. It is also on the product page Gumroad shows you straight after paying.

  1. Open the receipt from Gumroad and copy the licence key. It looks like XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX.
  2. Click the Magic Scraper icon in Chrome's toolbar, on any ordinary web page, then Upgrade to Pro at the bottom of the popup.
  3. Paste the key into the box and press Activate.

That is the whole thing. The chip at the top of the popup turns from Free to Pro, the badge disappears from drill-down, and the upgrade button is replaced by the byline. There is no account to create and nothing to log into.

On your other computers

Paste the same key. There is no device limit, nothing to deactivate when you change machine, and no count to run out of. Keep the receipt email; it is the copy of the key you will want later.

What you just unlocked

Drill-down on every row, rather than the first three — the extension opens the page behind each row, takes the fields you confirmed on the first one, and merges them back as new columns. Chain as many levels as the data is buried under. Everything else was already free and stays that way.

The sandbox has a three-level chain in it if you want to see what a deep run looks like before pointing it at something that matters.

If the key will not activate

Email support@magicscraper.app with the key and the exact wording of the message the popup showed, and I will sort it out. Everything else is on the support page, including the 7-day refund.
