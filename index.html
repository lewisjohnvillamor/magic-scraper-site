What it does

No selectors to write, no account to make.

Finds the data itself

Tables, card grids, repeated lists, schema.org markup, shadow DOM and same-origin iframes. If it picks the wrong block, cycle to the next one.

Point at it yourself

For layouts nothing can guess: click the rows, shift-click extra elements, and merge several areas of a page into one table.

Crawls pagination

Finds the Next control, follows URL or click pagination, handles infinite scroll, and backs off politely when a site pushes back.

Follows each row

Drill into every row's detail page and merge those fields back in as extra columns — several levels deep if you need it.

Shapes the data

Rename columns, drop rows and columns, filter, and get a one-line read on which columns came back mostly empty.

Remembers each site

Speed, page limits, excluded columns and renames are saved per site, so a weekly scrape is not a weekly setup.

Questions

Do I need the paid version?

Only if you scrape more than three pages at a time, or want the detail page behind every row. Everything else is free and stays free. Try it on the sites you actually care about first — that's what the free limits are for.

Does it work on any site?

Most. It reads what your browser has already rendered, so anything you can see, it can usually take. It cannot reach closed shadow roots, cross-origin iframes, content only a private API returns, or sites behind bot challenges — those are limits of the browser, not of this extension.

Is scraping a site allowed?

That's between you and the site. This is a tool, like a browser or a spreadsheet. Check the terms of the sites you use, respect robots.txt where it applies, and don't hammer anyone — the speed setting exists for that reason.

What happens if I switch computers?

Paste the same licence key. One key works on every browser and machine you use; there is no device limit and nothing to deactivate.

Who makes this?

One person. That means replies come from someone who wrote the code, and also that support is email rather than a helpdesk.
