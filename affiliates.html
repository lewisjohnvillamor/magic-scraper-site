What's true to say

Magic Scraper finds the table or list on the page and names the columns; it follows Next through every page for free, and exports CSV, Excel or JSON, or pastes into a Google Sheet. No account, no credits, no per-row limits. It runs in the browser, so nothing scraped is sent to us. Pro ($19 once, $25 after the first 100) opens every row's own page and adds its details as columns (free does three), runs one setup over a whole list of pages, compares a run with the last one or with a file, runs on a schedule, and sends rows to Google Sheets or a webhook.

Magic Snippers saves full page screenshots of every page of a list, or of every item in it, named and filed, inside the user's own login; scrolling videos and PDFs too. Pro ($19 once) removes the per-run limits.

The rules

  • Say it's an affiliate link wherever you share it.
  • No ads on our names. Don't bid on “Magic Scraper”, “Magic Snippers” or misspellings of them.
  • No coupon, deal or cashback sites, and no buying through your own link.
  • No spam: no unsolicited messages, comment spam or fake reviews.
  • Only claim what it does. Show what you've tried yourself; don't promise it works on a particular site (it isn't built for LinkedIn or Google Maps, for example).

Affiliates who break these are removed. If a customer is refunded, the commission on that sale is taken back.

Questions

When do I get paid?

Gumroad holds each sale for 7 days, then pays your balance on the schedule you choose (weekly, monthly or quarterly), by bank transfer or PayPal. Gumroad's minimum payout is $100, a little higher in a few countries, so at about $8.30 a sale your first payment comes after about twelve sales.

Does a free install count?

No. You're paid when someone you sent buys Pro within 30 days of clicking your link. Most people try it free first, which is why the link lands them on our site to install it.

Do I get both products?

Yes. Approved affiliates are added to Magic Scraper Pro and Magic Snippers Pro, both at 50%.

Can I use a link shortener or my own discount code?

A shortener is fine as long as it points to your gumroad.com/a/… link. For a discount for your audience, ask us at support.

Where do I see my sales?

In Gumroad, under your affiliated products: clicks you sent, sales and what you've earned.
