Comparison

A Data Miner alternative, honestly.

Data Miner is a capable, long-running scraper extension with a large library of ready-made "recipes". Here is what differs, where Magic Scraper is the better fit, and where Data Miner still is.

Install for Chrome — free Install for Edge Free: detection, every page, Excel/CSV/JSON export.

What's the same, and what isn't

Both find tables and lists on a page, follow pagination and export to Excel or CSV from inside the browser.

The difference is how you pay. Data Miner's free plan covers 500 pages a month and its paid plans are monthly subscriptions with page credits. Magic Scraper's detection, crawling and export are free with no page limit; Pro, for drill-down past three rows and the extras, is paid once.

When to keep Data Miner

When a ready-made recipe already exists for the site you need, and you would rather pick it than set anything up yourself. Data Miner's recipe library is large and that is a real advantage. If its monthly plan fits your volume, there's no need to switch.

Reasonable questions

Is Magic Scraper free like Data Miner's free plan?

Its free version has no monthly page limit: detection, crawling every page and export to CSV, XLSX and JSON are free. Drill-down past three rows and a few extras are part of Pro, a one-time purchase.

Does Magic Scraper use recipes?

No. It detects the table or list on the page itself and names the columns from the page's markup; a manual picker covers awkward layouts. Your setup is remembered per site.

Can I use both?

Yes. They don't conflict.
