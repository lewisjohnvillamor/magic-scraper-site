Only the leads you don’t have yet

Collect the directory, open the Changes tab and choose Compare with a file — your CRM export, as CSV, Excel or JSON. Every row on the page that is not in your file is marked new, and Export the changes saves just those. Rows are matched on a column both have, usually the phone number or website, and a phone matches however it is written. The file is read on your computer and never uploaded.

Put the directory on a schedule and the same comparison shows the businesses that have joined since last time. Both are part of Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase: $19 at the founding price for the first 100 buyers, $25 after that, with every update included.

One trade, many towns

Directories split results by category and place: plumbers in Leeds, plumbers in York, electricians in both. Set the list up once and run it over every results page — they merge into one sheet with a column saying which search each lead came from.

Keeping it clean

Keep to company-level fields — the business, its address, main phone and website — and leave out named people unless you have a clear reason. Whether you may collect from a directory is a matter of its terms; contacting the businesses you find is covered by the rules on marketing calls and email where you are, such as GDPR and the ePrivacy rules in the EU, PECR in the UK and CAN-SPAM in the US. What it isn’t

It is not a contact database and it doesn’t guess or look up email addresses: it collects what a public page shows you, from the pages you choose. That is also why it costs nothing to run — there are no credits and no per-lead price.

Reasonable questions

Is there a free lead scraper?

Magic Scraper is a free browser extension that turns a public directory into a spreadsheet: every listing on every page, with the columns named, exported to Excel or CSV with no account and no per-lead cost.

How do I build a B2B lead list for free?

Find a public list of the businesses you want to reach, such as a trade directory, a chamber or association member directory or an exhibitor list, open it in Chrome or Edge, click Magic Scraper, crawl every page and export to Excel.

Can it find email addresses?

It collects the emails a directory shows, in the list or on each business’s own page with drill-down. It doesn’t guess or look up addresses that aren’t on the page.

Can it remove leads I already have?

Yes, with Pro. Compare with a file sets your CRM export against the directory and marks only the rows that aren’t in it; Export the changes saves just those.

Is scraping business directories for leads legal?

It depends on the directory’s terms and the rules where you are, especially for contacting people. Keep to company-level fields and read our plain-words guide, which covers the EU, UK and US. It is not legal advice.
