Support

A real person reads this inbox · usually answered within a day or two

Email support@magicscraper.app and it reaches someone who can actually fix what you hit. No ticket number, no first-line script, no queue.

Using Magic Snippers, the screenshot extension? Its answers are further down; the same inbox handles both.

What to put in the email

The single most useful thing is the URL of the page you were scraping. Almost every problem is specific to one page's markup, and without the address there is nothing to look at. If the page is behind a login, say so and describe what the rows look like instead.

Then, briefly: what you expected, what happened, and whether the status line in the popup said anything. That is usually enough.

Things worth trying first

It found the wrong block Open Wrong list? and press Another table to cycle through the other candidates it ranked. If none of them is right, use Pick manually: click the rows, then shift-click anything else you want as a column.
Columns are called Text, Text 2… The page has no headers to name them from — common on card grids. Double-click a column header to rename it; the names are remembered for that site.
The crawl stopped early The popup says why. "No further Next link" means the site ran out of pages. A 429 means the site is rate-limiting you: set Speed to Gentle and try again. More in why scrapers stop at page 1.
Rows are still there after Clear Infinite scroll adds rows to the page itself, not to the extension. Use the Reload page button that appears after clearing.
Nothing is detected at all Try the sandbox. If it works there and not on your site, that is worth an email — send the URL. First, the checks in when a scraper finds nothing.
Something changed after an update The changelog lists what each update changed, including anything removed or moved to Pro.

Things no in-browser tool can reach

Closed shadow roots, data that only a private API returns, and PDFs in the browser's viewer. These are limits of the browser, not of this extension, and no amount of fixing on my end will change them. Two come close and have a way round: a bot challenge pauses the run until you've solved it yourself and pressed Resume, and a table shown from another site in a frame opens in a tab of its own from the panel's Open it on its own. If your page is one of these, I would rather tell you that than take your money.

Licence keys

Your key arrives by email in your Gumroad receipt, the moment you buy. Paste it into the extension and click Activate — there is no account and no login. The same key works on every browser and computer you use, with no device limit and nothing to deactivate when you change machine.

If a key will not activate, send me the key and the exact wording of the message the popup showed. Cannot find the key? Search your inbox for the Gumroad receipt, or email from the address you bought with and I will dig it out.

Refunds

Seven days, no questions asked. Email with your licence key. Purchases are processed by Gumroad as merchant of record, so the refund itself is issued through them.

Need something scraped for you?

A site behind a login, one that fights back, or a scrape that has to run every morning without you — that is a project rather than a tool, and we take those on. Send the site, the fields you need, how often, and the budget and deadline you have in mind: support@magicscraper.app. There is more detail on the home page.

Asking for a feature

Post it on the request board, or vote for one that is already there. The most-wanted requests are built first, and you can be told when yours ships.

Magic Snippers

Magic Snippers saves full page screenshots, scrolling videos and images of one page, of every page of a list, or of every item a list links to, inside your own login. Email support@magicscraper.app with the address of the page and what the panel said. The panel's ⓘ button explains every permission it uses.

Chrome says it “started debugging this browser” That bar appears while a whole-page screenshot or a scrolling video is captured: it is how an extension can photograph the whole page. It covers only the tab being captured and goes away when the capture ends. Pressing Cancel on it stops the capture.
Chrome asks to allow a site The first time Magic Snippers works on a site, or opens the items of a list on another site, Chrome asks you to allow it. Click Allow; it only ever asks for the sites you use it on.
It clicked the wrong “Next”, or stopped early The panel says why it stopped. In an item run you can name the button yourself, by its text or a CSS selector: the list's own Next in Scan options, each item's in Pages inside each item. In This page and the next pages it is found automatically; if it picks the wrong one, email the page's address.
Some items failed Press Retry failed when the run ends. A link that downloads a file instead of opening a page is reported as failed, and the _index.csv says which.
An image wasn't saved Images smaller than Smallest image (100 px by default) are left out, so lower it to include icons and thumbnails. Images drawn by CSS backgrounds, or that exist only inside the page's code, can't be saved.
A PDF won't capture Open the PDF in Chrome first, then capture it. A PDF the site sends as a download, rather than showing it, can't be captured yet.
The file names are wrong Open Change the file name (or Change the folder and file names for an item run): the preview above it shows exactly what will be saved.

Magic Snippers Pro is a separate licence from Magic Scraper Pro, bought and activated the same way (Upgrade → paste the key → Activate), with the same seven-day refund. More on the Magic Snippers page; what it stores and sends is in the privacy policy.

Reporting a bug

Email support@magicscraper.app and include the URL. That is the one detail that makes a report actionable.
