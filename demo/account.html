About this practice page

One table out of five, every page of it

Customer portals, supplier accounts and admin dashboards put several tables on one screen — orders, invoices, shipments, messages — each with its own page numbers. The job is almost always one of them: every invoice for the accountant, every shipment for a claim. The task here: all 40 invoices across their four pages, and not one row from the other tables.

Why it is tricky

The orders table above the invoices has a Next button that looks exactly like the invoices’ one, and it comes first. A tool that takes “the Next button on the page” pages the orders and ends up with ten invoices. The other tables add their own twists: shipments page with a button that has no address, returns with Show more, and messages have no pages at all. Since version 2.1, Magic Scraper uses the Next button that belongs to the table you chose.

How to do it

Magic Scraper opens on the invoices, the biggest table. For another one, use Wrong list? → Another table until it is outlined, or Pick manually. Then press Start crawling. To collect all five tables, do them one after another and export each.

What you should get

Invoices: 40 rows, INV-2026-1301 to INV-2026-1340, all different, no order numbers among them. Orders: 18. Shipments: 15. Returns: 12. Messages: 5.
