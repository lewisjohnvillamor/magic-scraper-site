GRIT

A short story in five pages. Mara is fourteen when her father breaks his ankle, and the bread still has to get up Harrow Hill: twenty lamp posts, one bicycle, every morning before sunrise.

Read GRIT (PDF, 5 pages)

For Magic Snippers: open the PDF, then in Screenshots choose This page. With Capture set to Whole page you get all five pages stacked as one image; with Scrolling video you get a video that scrolls through them, top to bottom. Chrome shows a PDF in its own viewer, so the extension reads the file itself and draws the pages with pdf.js.
Expected: one image of 5 pages, or one video from the title to THE END.
