Practising a deep drill-down

The catalogue goes three links deep, so you can build a real chain rather than a single hop:

LevelPageFollowWhat you get
StartCatalogue the product nameName, price, rating, stock, SKU
1Product page About the maker →SKU, availability, material, weight, warranty
2Maker profile Materials & sourcing →Founded, based in, workshop size, lead time
3Sourcing —Certification, recycled content, last audited

Both follow-links are labelled identically on every page, so a chain you confirm once on the first product works for all 54. Nine rows through all three levels yields 27 detail columns.

Every page also carries the things a real shop carries — a nav bar, a filter rail, a footer full of link lists. Those are there on purpose: a detector that only works on a page containing nothing but the table has not really been tested.
