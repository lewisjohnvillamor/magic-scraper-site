About this practice page

A local directory, and the lead list behind it

Trade directories, chamber of commerce member lists and “find a tradesperson” sites all look like this: a short card per business, and the useful details — email, website, address, opening hours — one click away on each business’s own profile. If you are building a list of businesses to contact, this is the job.

Watch it done on this page: “Turn any business directory into a lead list in 1 click” (YouTube)

Why it is two steps

The list itself only has a name, a trade and a phone number, over three pages. Everything else is on the profiles, one page per business. Magic Scraper does both in one run: it collects every listing, then opens each profile and adds its details as columns.

How to do it

Open Magic Scraper on this page and press Start crawling to collect all three pages. Then, on the Crawl tab, turn on drill-down, preview the first profile and press Use this for all rows. The free version reads the first three profiles so you can see the result; Pro reads all of them. With Pro, Compare with a file takes your CRM export and marks the businesses you do not have yet.

What you should get

24 businesses, and with the profiles read, six more columns: Address, Phone, Email, Website, Opening hours and Established. Keep to business details and check the site’s terms: is it legal to scrape a business directory? The whole workflow is in building a B2B lead list.
