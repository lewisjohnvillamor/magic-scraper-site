About this practice page

A clearance list where the important number is outside the list

End-of-line pages, outlet sections and trade promotions often work like this one: a table of products at their old price, and a banner above it that changes everything — here, “−35% applies to every line below”. If you are comparing prices or deciding what to buy, the price you would pay is the one you want, and the page never writes it down.

What a scraper can and can’t know

Any good tool gets the table: ten lines with Product, SKU, Category, Was and Left, plus the product link. No automatic detection can know that the banner applies to the rows: it is a sentence written for people, and the connection between it and the rows exists only in the reader’s head. A scraper that silently invented a “Now” column would be worse than one that left it out. What you can do is tell it.

Pointing at it yourself

In Magic Scraper, open Wrong list? and choose Pick manually. Click one of the rows, then shift-click the −35% in the banner, and press Use selection: the ten rows come back with the discount as a column of its own, the same value on every row. That puts the rule in the data, which is the part that matters — in a week the sheet will be the only record of it.

Turning it into a price

The column holds “−35%”, not the price you would pay; the arithmetic is one formula in the sheet. With Was in column D, put this in a spare column and fill down:

=VALUE(SUBSTITUTE(D2,"$",""))*(1-35%)

In pandas it is one line once the table is read:

import pandas as pd
df = pd.read_html("https://magicscraper.app/demo/awkward")[0]
df["Now"] = (df["Was"].str.lstrip("$").astype(float) * 0.65).round(2)

If you track the page on a schedule, keep the picked discount in the saved setup: a new banner then shows up as a change on every row, where a table read without it would never notice.

A correct result

10 rows. Walnut Tray was $429.00, so it is $278.85 after the discount; Jute Tray, Bone goes from $488.00 to $317.20. The Left column is a count, from 1 to 18, and arrives as numbers.
