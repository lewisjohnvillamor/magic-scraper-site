About this practice page

A clearance list where the key number is outside the list

Outlet sections and trade promotions often look like this: a table of products at their old price, and a banner above it that changes everything — here, “−35% applies to every line below”. The price you would actually pay is never written down.

Why it is tricky

Any good tool finds the ten lines of the table. No tool can know on its own that the banner applies to them — that connection is written for people. So Magic Scraper lets you point at it.

How to do it

Open Magic Scraper, then Wrong list? → Pick manually. Click one of the rows, shift-click the −35% in the banner, and press Use selection: the ten rows come back with the discount as a column of its own. To turn it into a price in your sheet, with Was in column D, put =VALUE(SUBSTITUTE(D2,"$",""))*(1-35%) in a spare column and fill down. Tracking the page on a schedule? Keep the picked discount in the saved setup, and a new banner shows up as a change on every row.

What you should get

10 rows with the discount on each. Walnut Tray was $429.00, so it is $278.85 after the discount; Jute Tray, Bone goes from $488.00 to $317.20.
