About this practice page

Volume pricing on each product’s own page

Trade and wholesale shops price by quantity: one price for one to nine, less for ten to forty-nine, less again for fifty or more. The list shows only “from $1.80”; the real price breaks are in a small table on each product’s page. If you are comparing suppliers, those breaks are the whole point.

Why it is tricky

Every product page has to be opened, and each product has several price breaks, so one product is not one row. Side by side as columns (“Quantity 1, Price 1, Quantity 2…”) they cannot be sorted or compared. What you want is one row per price break, with the product’s details on each — and that is what Magic Scraper gives you.

How to do it

Open Magic Scraper on this page. On the Crawl tab, turn on drill-down and preview the first product: it shows the specifications and finds the price-break table. Tick Every row of its table — one row each and run it. The free version reads the first three products so you can see the result; Pro reads them all.

What you should get

12 rows — three breaks for each of four products — each with the SKU, finish, delivery time, quantity, unit price and discount. The steel hinge runs from $2.40 for 1–9 to $1.80 for 50 or more; a dash in the first break’s discount means none.
