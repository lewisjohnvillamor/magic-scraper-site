Reasonable questions

Which websites does Magic Scraper work on?

Most pages that show a list or a table: it finds the repeating rows on whatever page you open. These guides are the sites we tested on 4 November 2026, with the result each gave, plus five sites it reads by name.

Why is my favourite site not here?

Either we have not written it up yet, or it showed our automated test browser a “verify you’re human” check, which a person’s own browser usually does not see. Guides for those are being recorded in a normal browser.

Does it work on LinkedIn, Google Maps or Zillow?

We do not promise those. They are built to resist automated reading, and Magic Scraper never tries to get past a check.
