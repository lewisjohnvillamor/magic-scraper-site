Inside your login, on your computer

Online screenshot services can only see a page behind your login if you hand them your session cookies. Magic Snippers doesn't need them: it works in the browser you're already signed in to, so dashboards, admin panels, member areas and client portals are captured exactly as you see them.

Reasonable questions

Does it work on sites I'm signed in to?

Yes — that's the point of running in your own browser. It sees exactly what you see, with your own session, and nothing about it is sent anywhere. It can't open Chrome's own pages, the Chrome Web Store or a new tab; Chrome blocks every extension there.

Why does Chrome say it “started debugging this browser”?

Chrome shows that bar whenever an extension uses its debugging interface, and it can't be hidden. It's the only way an extension can make a tab as tall as the whole page to photograph it in one go, or record the page as it scrolls. Magic Snippers attaches to the one tab being captured, only while it captures, and detaches when it's done. Pressing Cancel on the bar stops the capture; nothing else happens.

Why does it ask for these permissions?

Reading the tab you click the icon on: to find the data, the links and the Next button; other sites need your OK, site by site. Debugger: whole-page screenshots and videos, as above. Tabs: to show which page it's working on and name each item's file; it doesn't read your history. Scripting: to run its own code in the page; none is fetched from the internet. Downloads: to save your files. Storage: your settings and licence key. Side panel: to stay open beside the page. The ⓘ button in the panel says the same. The privacy policy, in full.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. What it reads and saves stays in your browser and your Downloads folder. The only thing that leaves your computer is a Pro licence key, which is checked with Gumroad.

Can I keep working while it captures?

Yes. Screenshots are taken in a background tab and videos in a separate window. You can also pop the panel out (⧉) into its own window and put it anywhere.

Is my Magic Scraper licence valid here?

No — they are separate products with separate licences. Each has a free version that does most of the job, so you can find out which one you need before paying.

How do I activate Pro?

Open the Magic Snippers panel, click Upgrade, paste the licence key from your Gumroad receipt and click Activate. It works on any browser you personally use.
