  • Job title and the listing link
  • Company, its profile link and logo

  • How long ago it was posted

Going further, and the limits

Salary and the full description are on each job’s page. Drill-down can open each one; free runs it on three rows.

Whether you may collect from a site is between you and that site: its terms, its robots.txt and data-protection law. Magic Scraper reads only what your browser already shows you, paces itself gently by default, and a “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab; it never tries to get past one.

Reasonable questions

Is there a free We Work Remotely scraper?

Yes. Magic Scraper reads We Work Remotely pages and exports CSV, Excel or JSON for free, with no account and no row cap. In our test on remote design jobs it found 18 rows and 17 columns on the first page.

Did you really test it?

Yes, on 4 November 2026, with the released extension, on the page shown in the clip. Sites change; if yours does not come out right, tell us and it gets looked at.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. It reads the page you are looking at, in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us; there is no server of ours to send it to.

What if We Work Remotely shows me a check?

A “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab. Magic Scraper never tries to get past one.
