Site guide · tested 4 November 2026

Scrape a Shopify store into a spreadsheet.

Open a shop’s collection page, click the icon, and every product comes out with its name, link, image and price. We tested Allbirds, which runs on Shopify; collection pages on other Shopify themes are laid out differently, so try yours.

Install for Chrome — free Install for Edge Free exports, no paywall on your data. No account.

Tested 4 November 2026 on Allbirds’ men’s shoes: 38 rows and 22 columns on the first page.

What comes out

  • Product link and image
  • Product name and colour
  • Price
  • Badges and colour counts, in plainly named columns you can leave out

How to do it

01

Open a collection

Any category or search page in the shop.

02

Click the icon

Every product on the page, one per row.

03

Tidy it

This page gave 22 columns, two of them mostly empty; click the headers you do not need.

04

Export

CSV, Excel or JSON.

Going further, and the limits

Sizes, stock and descriptions are on each product’s page. Drill-down can open each one and add them; free runs it on three rows. Shops ask for email sign-ups in pop-ups; close one before you click the icon.

Whether you may collect from a site is between you and that site: its terms, its robots.txt and data-protection law. Magic Scraper reads only what your browser already shows you, paces itself gently by default, and a “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab; it never tries to get past one.

Reasonable questions

Is there a free Shopify stores scraper?

Yes. Magic Scraper reads Shopify stores pages and exports CSV, Excel or JSON for free, with no account and no row cap. In our test on Allbirds’ men’s shoes it found 38 rows and 22 columns on the first page.

Did you really test it?

Yes, on 4 November 2026, with the released extension, on the page shown in the clip. Sites change; if yours does not come out right, tell us and it gets looked at.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. It reads the page you are looking at, in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us; there is no server of ours to send it to.

What if Shopify stores shows me a check?

A “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab. Magic Scraper never tries to get past one.
