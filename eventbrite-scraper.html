Scrape Eventbrite events into a spreadsheet.

Open Eventbrite’s events for a city, click the icon, and every event comes out with its dates, venue address and coordinates, read from the page’s own event data.

Install for Chrome — free Install for Edge Free exports, no paywall on your data. No account.

Tested 4 November 2026 on events in San Francisco: 40 rows and 15 columns on the first page. Download the GIF

What comes out

  • Name and the event’s link
  • Start and End dates
  • Location, Street, City, Region, Postal Code and Country
  • Latitude and Longitude
  • Whether it is online or in person, and its image

Going further, and the limits

Ticket prices are on each event’s page, not in this list. Drill-down can open each event and add what its page shows; free runs it on three rows.

Whether you may collect from a site is between you and that site: its terms, its robots.txt and data-protection law. Magic Scraper reads only what your browser already shows you, paces itself gently by default, and a “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab; it never tries to get past one.

Reasonable questions

Is there a free Eventbrite scraper?

Yes. Magic Scraper reads Eventbrite pages and exports CSV, Excel or JSON for free, with no account and no row cap. In our test on events in San Francisco it found 40 rows and 15 columns on the first page.

Did you really test it?

Yes, on 4 November 2026, with the released extension, on the page shown in the clip. Sites change; if yours does not come out right, tell us and it gets looked at.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. It reads the page you are looking at, in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us; there is no server of ours to send it to.

What if Eventbrite shows me a check?

A “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab. Magic Scraper never tries to get past one.
