Scrape Rotten Tomatoes into a spreadsheet.

Open a Rotten Tomatoes browse page, click the icon, and every movie comes out with its link, poster and scores. Start crawling presses Load more for you.

Install for Chrome — free Install for Edge Install for Firefox Free exports, no paywall on your data. No account.

Tested 4 November 2026 on movies in theaters: 28 rows and 10 columns on the first page; 87 rows over 3 pages with Start crawling. Download the GIF

What comes out

  • Title, link and poster
  • The critics and audience percentages
  • Whether a score is fresh, or not in yet
  • Some columns arrive with plain names such as Text 2; double-click a header to rename it

How to do it

01

Open a browse page

In theaters, at home, coming soon, or any filter.

02

Click the icon

The movies on screen, one per row.

03

Load more

Press Start crawling. It presses Load more and waits for each batch: in our test, three rounds and 87 movies.

04

Export

CSV, Excel or JSON.

Going further, and the limits

Reviews and full credits are on each movie’s page. Drill-down can open each one; free runs it on three rows.

Whether you may collect from a site is between you and that site: its terms, its robots.txt and data-protection law. Magic Scraper reads only what your browser already shows you, paces itself gently by default, and a “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab; it never tries to get past one.

Reasonable questions

Is there a free Rotten Tomatoes scraper?

Yes. Magic Scraper reads Rotten Tomatoes pages and exports CSV, Excel or JSON for free, with no account and no row cap. In our test on movies in theaters it found 28 rows and 10 columns on the first page.

Did you really test it?

Yes, on 4 November 2026, with the released extension, on the page shown in the clip. Sites change; if yours does not come out right, tell us and it gets looked at.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. It reads the page you are looking at, in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us; there is no server of ours to send it to.

What if Rotten Tomatoes shows me a check?

A “verify you’re human” check stops the run and hands you the tab. Magic Scraper never tries to get past one.
