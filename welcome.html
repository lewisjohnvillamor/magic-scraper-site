Magic Scraper is installed.

Two minutes, three steps, and you will have your first spreadsheet.

  1. Pin the icon. New extensions hide in the puzzle-piece menu at the top right of the browser. Click the puzzle piece, then the pin beside Magic Scraper, and it stays in the toolbar.

On Edge the puzzle piece is the same; the pin is an eye icon, Show in toolbar.

  1. Try it where it is meant to work. Open the practice shop below and click the icon. Nine products are found and named without you doing anything; press Export CSV and that is your first file.
Open the practice shop
  1. Then your own page. Open the page with the list you want and click the icon. If it picked the wrong list, open Wrong list? and press Another table; if it picked none, choose Pick manually there and click one of the rows.

Three things worth knowing

Every page, not just this one. The Crawl tab finds the page's Next button and collects every page of results into one sheet. Free, with no page limit.

The page behind each row. When each row links to a page of its own, drill-down opens them one by one and adds what it finds as extra columns — emails from profile pages, specs from product pages. How drill-down works.

Keep it open. The pin at the top of the popup moves it into the side panel, where it stays while you click around the page.

What you scrape goes only where you send it, and there is no account. If something does not work on a site, tell me which one and it gets looked at.
